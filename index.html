About

The person behind Anders Apps

Hey — I'm Isaiah.

I build apps because I care about what people actually use every day. Not just tools that look good, but ones that quietly become part of someone's life.

By day, I work in nonprofit tech—helping systems work better for real people. That's shaped how I think about everything I build: it should be simple, clear, and genuinely helpful.

I'm also a husband and a dad, and my faith is a big part of how I approach my work. I don't want to just make apps—I want to make things that help people know God, stay grounded, and live with intention.

Anders Apps is just that. Not a company chasing scale. Just a place to build things that matter.

Why this exists

A few things I believe

01
Ship something real

A finished, imperfect thing beats a perfect idea that never ships. I'd rather put something out and improve it.

02
Build what you'd actually use

The best filter for any feature is whether I'd genuinely use it myself. If the answer is no, it probably doesn't belong.

03
Slow is intentional

The world moves fast. The best apps I can make are ones that help people slow down, not ones that compete for attention.

04
Details matter quietly

Good design doesn't announce itself. It just feels right. I care a lot about the stuff most people won't consciously notice.
